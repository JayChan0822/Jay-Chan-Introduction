我是陈俊，上海音乐学院电影音乐制作硕士在读。擅长国风、流行、管弦等风格的音乐。

曾参与《逆水寒》《燕云十六声》《明日之后》《一梦江湖》《花亦山心之月》等多项游戏配乐制作；

2025-2026 年间参与陈致逸 DG 发行个人专辑《漫长的告白》制谱、录音与音乐编辑，并为其《旧友·新声》音乐会担任编配与制谱。

2025 年，原创管弦乐《水乡·记忆》入选全国研究生原创作品交响音乐会。

致力于融合传统管弦与前沿音乐美学，重塑沉浸式听觉体验。

I am Jay Chan, an M.A. candidate in Film Music Production at the Shanghai Conservatory of Music, specializing in Chinese Guofeng, pop, and orchestral styles.

I have worked on the soundtracks of multiple video games, including Justice Online (逆水寒), Where Winds Meet (燕云十六声), LifeAfter (明日之后), A Dream of Jiang Hu (一梦江湖), and The Moonlit Oath (花亦山心之月).

In 2025–2026, I contributed music engraving, recording, and music editing to composer Yu-Peng Chen's solo album The Poem of Life, released on Deutsche Grammophon, and served as arranger and engraver for his concert "Old Friends|New Voices" (旧友·新声).

In 2025, my original orchestral work Silk Rain & Willow Shadows (水乡·记忆) was selected for the National Symphony Concert of Original Works by Graduate Students.

My artistic mission lies at the intersection of traditional orchestration and cutting-edge music, dedicated to reshaping the boundaries of immersive auditory experiences.
